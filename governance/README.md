# AI Governance Framework and Control Mapping

This directory contains the project's governance framework register, proposed organisational control register, framework-to-control mappings, evidence references and governance analysis outputs.

## Scope

The project organises relevant Indian and international AI governance frameworks into a structured reference register and maps them to proposed organisational controls.

The governance work includes:

- 9 framework and regulatory register entries.
- 16 proposed governance controls.
- 76 framework-to-control mappings.
- 19 evidence references.
- Governance mapping and evidence validation outputs.

## Framework coverage

The register includes references to India's AI Governance Guidelines, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Rules, the EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 42005, OECD AI Principles and a TRAI draft consultation.

## Control status

15 controls are classified as **Proposed – not assessed**.

One item is classified as **Policy watch – draft**.

These classifications do not imply that controls have been implemented, independently tested or assessed for effectiveness.

## Interpretation

Framework-to-control mappings are analytical associations developed for this project. They are not legal opinions or formal compliance determinations.

Evidence references provide traceability to supporting sources and documentation; they should not be interpreted as proof of operational control effectiveness.

The purpose of this work is to demonstrate structured governance analysis, accountability-oriented documentation and traceability.
